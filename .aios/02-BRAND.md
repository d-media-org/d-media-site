# Brand

## Purpose
Document repository-backed brand rules for `d . media`.

## Scope
Covers naming, tone, positioning, brand assets, typography, and public voice evidenced in repository files.

## Rules
- Always spell the brand as `d . media`.
- Use `d media` and `d-media` only as alternate names in SEO/schema contexts where already present.
- Use `Panton` as the brand typeface family.
- Keep communication restrained, clear, systematic, and practical.
- Do not change visual identity or brand assets without explicit human instruction.

## Brand Philosophy
Repository copy positions `d . media` as a studio focused on clarity, order, consistency, real use, and systems rather than isolated visuals. Bulgarian copy uses plural studio voice such as `работим`, `създаваме`, and `подреждаме`; English copy uses `we`.

## Examples
- Correct: `d . media creates brand identities and websites as working systems.`
- Incorrect: `D Media`, `D.Media`, `d.media`, unless quoting an external handle.
- Correct: maintain the quieter system-focused tone used in `site-content.ts`.
- Incorrect: adding hype-heavy campaign language without a source.
